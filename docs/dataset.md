# Benchmark and Annotation Plan

The benchmark target is approximately 150-200 manually checked Indian packaged-food products. It must be populated from traceable product and packaging evidence; no synthetic records may be presented as measured data.

Each record will include product image, product name, brand, category, raw claim, claim type, nutrition fields, applicable sourced rule, comparator when required, expected verdict, evidence notes, and annotation notes.

Annotations will be independently reviewed, disagreements recorded, and quality-checked before evaluation. Training and testing will be split by brand or product family, never by random product rows, to prevent near-duplicate packaging leakage. The split manifest will be versioned with the benchmark.

Benchmark size, accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and false-approval results are **NOT YET MEASURED**.
